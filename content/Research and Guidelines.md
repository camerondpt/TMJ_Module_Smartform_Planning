## Continuing Ed Courses (Medbridge)

These three courses from Medbridge do a fantastic job of walking through examination, evaluation, and classification of TMD (along with treatment). They rely heavily on the above classification systems.
- [Orofacial Pain: Introduction, History and Examination](https://www.medbridge.com/courses/details/orofacial-pain-introduction-history-and-examination-anne-harrison-physical-therapy-orthopedics)
- [Evaluation and Diagnostic Classification of TMD](https://www.medbridge.com/courses/details/evaluation-and-diagnostic-classification-of-tmd-anne-harrison-physical-therapy-orthopedics)
- [Treatment of Temporomandibular Disorders and Headache](https://www.medbridge.com/courses/details/treatment-of-the-patient-with-temporomandibular-disorders-and-or-headache-anne-harrison-physical-therapy-geriatrics)
